# Unresolved questions

Draft implementation descriptions do not adopt normative profiles. Their
decision, protocol and conformance work follows LIP-0001.

| Question | Location and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Adoption and coexistence of landlord, locator and action-service profiles | LIP-0004/0005/0006 remain Draft; historical v0.1 requirements and evidence retain their own meaning. |
| Common action vocabulary versus wallet-specific encoding and limits | [LIP-0006 open questions](lips/LIP-0006-grant-gated-actions.md#open-questions); cross-language canonical rules and complete vectors precede normative interoperability claims. |
| Further locator networks and endpoint discovery | [LIP-0005 open questions](lips/LIP-0005-op-return-locator.md#5-open); current closed name schemas do not acquire blob/tracker fields. |
| Name-action continuation, remote transport, broker routing and non-coin budgets | LIP-0006 specifies the implemented wallet v1 choices; extensions need separate action/effect contracts. |
| Deployment qualification, key/UID provisioning and journal migration/recovery | LIP-0006; mainnet configuration support and same-UID fixtures do not establish actual cross-user custody or anti-rollback guarantees. |
| Booking-to-wallet terms and an agent-controlled small-wallet tier | LIP-0006; incompatible byte formats cannot be substituted, and refill/lease policy needs an explicit contract. |

Existing questions for single use and detached grants remain in LIP-0002 and
LIP-0003. This index does not change their status or the preserved evidence.

Whether an attestation is mandatory for every child with textual scope is proposed and not decided. Proposed and not decided: structured scope, a per-grant depth, on-chain budget enforcement, a required notary attestation for textual scope. A child is signed with the key the parent names as recipient; whether a re-delegating agent needs a grant key of its own, separate from its channel key, is open. Both owners concur. | Grant v4 exact encoding and resource accounting | [LIP-0008](lips/LIP-0008-grant-v4.md); signed limits and matching contract are the proposed design, with units, numeric bounds and reservation rules still requiring exact profiles. |
| Issuer history and grantee owner proof | LIP-0008 pins the landlord name's registration and owner key and walks the name's history through the transaction index: a changed owner or `swarma` since the committed block refuses on the active chain, without a journalled closure, and a renewal does not (decision of 2026-11-11). The start point of the history constraint and history proofs for a verifier without a full node remain open. | Consent, renewal and channel updates | An issuer name update requires reissuance only when it changes the owner or `swarma`. Grantee renewal/rotation under the same owner keeps the grant and checks current listed keys. Exact consent and replacement-grant bytes remain open. |

signer is implemented first. The signer service capability still does not
authorize arbitrary sub-delegation.

## Second design review

| Question | Location and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Issuer registration provisioning and renewal | LIP-0008 binds registration and owner key in grant, agent pointer and independent verifier pins. Renewal keeps all three. Pin provisioning and the approximately 220-day key-access reminder need an operational profile; expiry uses chain state. |
| Term, period and payment after a reorganisation | LIP-0008 sections 2.1 and 2.2 (decisions of 2026-11-11): one grant per hire for the whole term in blocks, the hour as a limit and payment period only; a displaced payment that stays valid is awaited for `N` blocks, conflicting spends revoke. `N`, the case of a payment still unconfirmed after `N` blocks and the unit of a period remain open. The reference booking service implements the earlier revoke-on-displacement rule. |
| Termination for violation | [LIP-0011](lips/LIP-0011-termination-for-violation.md): rules hash and moderator in the contract, signed verdict records, a reference in the seal spend, refund as a contract term (decisions of 2026-11-11). Proposed by the draft and not decided: the chain of previous verdicts, the extra record fields, action `0x03`, the commitment formula and prefix, the reader's checks and the four evidence states. Record bytes and domains, how violations are counted, the request-hash definition per service, a salt, the termination threshold as a contract term, moderator-key replacement and the fee source remain open. Nothing is implemented. |
| Notary | [LIP-0012](lips/LIP-0012-notary.md): an additional link that certifies a grant and can revoke it; for a child grant it certifies that the child does not exceed its parent; an agent or a person (decisions of 2026-11-11). Proposed by the draft and not decided: the separate attestation with its own seal, revocation through that seal, the signed requirement in a grant, the admission step and every field. Open: bytes and domains, the anchor, how a notary is pinned, durable closure or chain-relative refusal, certification by another notary after a withdrawal. Nothing is implemented in a grant profile. |
| Typed signing and migration | Static audit at chain-lab 839bdcca found self-recomputed authenticated contract hashes in both issuer paths, with no demonstrated oracle. Landlord/provider/detached-v2 application domains need versioned matched signer/verifier migration; standard NIP-01 and transaction preimages remain unchanged. Exact tags/bytes/vectors are open. |
| Issuer-branch mempool proof and recovery | LIP-0008 permanently closes after validated issuer evidence, including replacement/eviction. Exact branch/evidence encoding, shared journal and anti-rollback recovery are open; ordinary grantee use and transient reads remain distinct. |
| Confirmation risk policy | Typed minimum depth uses MAX of signed minimum and operator floor. Chain/evidence classes and value-dependent tiers need profiles; no cross-chain finality equivalence or numerical tiers are adopted. |
| T/L/A and payment dependency | W explicitly permits validated unconfirmed linkage/payment dependencies; L requires confirmed inputs and separate seal/anchor stages. Namecoin anchors spend only NMC outputs; BTC/LTC keep separate proofs. Name-output migration/self-reference, payment control, consent, fees and exact proofs remain open. No implementation or latency result. |
| Topology, no-block-wait selection and operator trust | LIP-0008 separates publication gating from offline verification. A no-block-wait profile needs explicit signed selection and evidence semantics; anchored failures never select it. Operator data trust is outside permission proof. |
| Per-seal derived keys | Typed issuer endorsement binds key/script/exact outpoint and context. LIP-0008 section 11.1 proposes BIP-32 derivation in a seal-only account for revocable-only seals on Namecoin (BIP-84 layout in W, BIP-44 in L, coin type 7); the single-use seal's two-branch output profile stays open. Non-hardened indices with the extended public key as an operator secret are the decided default (2026-11-11). Namecoin Core descriptor import, device support, custody/recovery and encoding remain open; funding/names/anchors still expose linkage. |
| Recipient modes and channel members | LIP-0008 section 3.1: by key without any name, or by name with the owner key and the request key read from the channel member. Only the Nostr channel is defined; other channel profiles and channel restriction per grant remain open. |
| Validity in block heights | LIP-0008 section 7.1 states grant windows in heights against the validated tip. A window may not open below the committed block's height, and invocation expiry stays in clock time (decided 2026-11-11). The recency bound for that block and the relation to the consent event's `created_at` remain open. |
| JCS and public conformance | Per-field exact integer range or decimal-string grammar must precede next-format RFC8785 adoption. Canonical/signature/error/state fixtures, runner and independent Swift implementation are plans. Historical v0.1 files remain frozen. |

Editorial and layout reconciliation, primary Core checks and historical wording
fixes are tracked for the next build; they are not silently applied to frozen
protocol files or evidence. LIP-0006's 32-record wallet journal limit remains a
laboratory bound, not a production retention policy. UCAN-derived pre-freeze
rules are expressed only as Swarma requirements in Draft LIP-0008. The planned
post-freeze spike, evaluator/resolver implementation and independent vectors
remain separate work and grant no implicit delegated issuance authority.
